# Privacy Policy for Expired

**Effective date: September 12, 2026**

Expired ("the App") is developed by Swift Studios. This policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used.

## Information we handle

Expired is designed not to collect or retain information that identifies you. Depending on the features you use, the App may process:

- **App and device diagnostics:** anonymised crash reports, performance information, and aggregate usage data used to improve reliability and features.
- **Subscription information:** Apple or the relevant app-store provider processes purchases and subscriptions. We do not receive your payment card details. We may receive limited, non-identifying entitlement information needed to provide paid features.
- **Information you submit to AI features:** if you choose to use an AI-powered feature, the text or other content required for that feature may be sent to the selected AI provider, such as Google Gemini or DeepSeek, to generate a response.

Please do not submit passwords, financial information, health information, or other sensitive personal information to an AI feature.

## How we use information

We use the information above only to provide, maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve Expired, and to verify access to subscription features. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information, and we do not use advertising networks.

## Third-party services

AI requests may be processed by Google Gemini or DeepSeek. Those providers may process requests according to their own privacy policies and terms. App-store providers process subscriptions and payments under their own policies. We aim to send only the information necessary for the feature you request.

Anonymised analytics and crash reporting may be provided by service providers configured by the App. These reports are intended not to identify you.

## Storage and retention

Expired may store app data locally on your device. We do not maintain a user-identifiable account database for Expired. Diagnostic or service-provider data is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above and according to the provider's policies.

## Your choices

You can stop using AI-powered features, disable available diagnostics or analytics controls, or delete the App and its local data. Subscription cancellation and refund requests are handled by the relevant app-store provider.

## Children

Expired is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Expired's features or services change. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
